Welcome to Our Community

Mubeezi Dating brings adults together from different countries, cultures and backgrounds. We want every member to feel respected and safe while looking for meaningful connections.

Our basic rule: Treat every member with honesty, dignity, kindness and respect.

1. Adults Only – 18+

Mubeezi Dating is exclusively for adults aged 18 and above.

  • Do not create an account if you are under 18.
  • Do not pretend to be an adult if you are underage.
  • Do not use another person's account.

2. Be Honest About Yourself

  • Use accurate personal and profile information.
  • Upload photographs that represent you.
  • Do not impersonate another person.
  • Do not create fake profiles to deceive other members.
  • Be honest about your intentions and relationship goals.

3. Respect Everyone

  • Respect other members' opinions and boundaries.
  • Do not insult, threaten or intimidate anyone.
  • Do not discriminate based on nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender or background.
  • Accept that another member may not be interested in you.
  • Do not repeatedly contact someone who has asked you to stop.

4. No Harassment or Abuse

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

  • Bullying or targeted harassment.
  • Threats of violence.
  • Blackmail or extortion.
  • Stalking or unwanted repeated contact.
  • Sharing private photographs or conversations without permission.
  • Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual content.

5. No Scams or Financial Fraud

  • Do not request money through dishonest or manipulative methods.
  • Do not promote investment, cryptocurrency or financial scams.
  • Do not pretend to be someone else to obtain money.
  • Do not share suspicious payment links.
  • Never pressure another member to send money.
Safety reminder: Be cautious if someone you have just met online asks for money, gifts, financial details or emergency assistance.

6. Appropriate Photos and Content

  • Upload photographs that you have permission to use.
  • Do not upload illegal or exploitative material.
  • Do not share intimate images without consent.
  • Do not upload content involving minors in a sexual or exploitative context.
  • Do not use photographs to impersonate other people.

7. Respect Privacy

  • Do not publish another member's private information.
  • Do not share passwords or account access.
  • Do not record or distribute private communications without permission.
  • Respect members who choose to keep certain information private.

8. No Spam or Platform Abuse

  • Do not send repetitive unwanted messages.
  • Do not advertise unrelated products or services without authorization.
  • Do not use bots or automated accounts to manipulate activity.
  • Do not attempt to hack, disrupt or misuse the platform.

9. Report and Block Unsafe Members

If another member makes you uncomfortable or violates these guidelines, use the available reporting and blocking features.

  • Report suspicious or abusive behaviour.
  • Block members who repeatedly disturb you.
  • Do not retaliate or threaten other members.
  • Contact support if you need additional assistance.

10. Meeting Someone in Person

Members who decide to meet offline should take reasonable precautions.

  • Meet in a public place.
  • Tell a trusted person where you are going.
  • Arrange your own transport.
  • Do not share your home address too early.
  • Leave if you feel unsafe or uncomfortable.

11. What Happens When Guidelines Are Violated?

Depending on the seriousness and circumstances of a violation, Mubeezi Dating may take action such as:

  • Removing inappropriate content.
  • Issuing a warning.
  • Temporarily restricting account features.
  • Suspending an account.
  • Permanently terminating an account.
  • Reporting serious unlawful conduct to appropriate authorities where required.

12. Our Commitment

We aim to create a community where adults can discover people, express interest, communicate and build meaningful relationships without harassment, deception or abuse.

Every member has a role in making Mubeezi Dating a better place.
