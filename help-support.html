Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Open the registration page, provide your details and complete your profile. Mubeezi Dating is for adults aged 18 and above.

How do I change my profile information?

Open your profile and select Edit Profile to update your information.

How do I block someone?

Open the member's profile and use the Block option if available. You can manage blocked members through Settings.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Subscription Management and follow the cancellation instructions for your subscription.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can request account deletion through the Account Deletion page in your settings.

How do I report an unsafe member?

Contact support and provide the relevant profile details and a description of your concern.
