Important: This is a draft privacy policy for Mubeezi Dating. It should be reviewed and finalized to reflect the actual services, data practices, and applicable privacy laws before the platform launches.

1. Introduction

Mubeezi Dating respects the privacy of its members and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, how it may be shared, and the choices available to members when using Mubeezi Dating.

The platform is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and above.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Mubeezi Dating may collect:

  • Account information, including email address and username.
  • Profile information, including display name, age, country and city.
  • Profile photographs and other photographs you choose to upload.
  • Interests, occupation, education and relationship preferences.
  • Messages and other communications sent through the platform.
  • Likes, matches, blocks and other account activity.
  • Subscription and payment status information.
  • Technical information such as device and browser information, where collected.
  • Information you provide when contacting customer support.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage member accounts.
  • Display profiles and help members discover other members.
  • Support likes, matches and messaging features.
  • Process and manage subscriptions and payments.
  • Respond to questions, complaints and support requests.
  • Protect members and investigate reports of abuse.
  • Improve platform performance and security.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Profile Visibility

Members choose profile information and photographs to share. Information displayed on public or discoverable profiles may be visible to other eligible members.

Members can use available privacy settings to manage profile visibility. Information already viewed, copied or captured by another person may not be recoverable.

5. Messages and Communications

Messages sent through Mubeezi Dating are processed to provide messaging services and support platform safety.

Members should avoid sharing passwords, financial details, identification documents or other highly sensitive information through ordinary messages.

6. Photographs and Verification

Profile photographs are used to display member profiles. Verification information, if collected, should be handled separately with appropriate security protections.

Identity documents and verification photographs should never be displayed publicly on member profiles.

7. Payments and Subscriptions

Paid services may involve payment providers such as Mobile Money, Visa or Mastercard processors.

Payment providers may process payment information under their own privacy policies. Mubeezi Dating should not store complete card numbers or security codes on its own systems.

8. Sharing Information

Personal information may be shared only where necessary to operate the service, provide requested features, protect members or comply with legal obligations.

Possible recipients may include:

  • Technology and hosting providers.
  • Authentication and database service providers.
  • Payment processors.
  • Customer support service providers.
  • Law enforcement or regulatory authorities where legally required.

We do not promise that information is never shared. Any sharing must be explained and handled according to applicable law.

9. Data Security

Mubeezi Dating aims to use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect member information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Members should protect their login details and report suspicious activity.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide services, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes and protect platform security.

When an account is deleted, information will be handled according to the applicable retention and deletion procedures. Some records may need to be retained where legally required.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, members may have rights to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Raise a complaint with an appropriate data protection authority.

Members can contact support to make a privacy request.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The platform may use cookies or similar technologies to support login sessions, security, preferences and service functionality. Any analytics or advertising technologies used should be disclosed and managed in accordance with applicable law.

13. Children's Privacy

Mubeezi Dating is strictly for adults aged 18 and above. We do not intend to provide dating services to children. Accounts discovered to belong to underage users may be restricted or removed.

14. International Data Transfers

Because Mubeezi Dating is intended for worldwide use, information may be processed or stored in countries different from the member's country of residence.

Where required, appropriate safeguards should be applied to international transfers.

15. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the platform develops or as legal requirements change. Members should review this page periodically.

Important changes should be communicated through appropriate platform notices or other suitable channels.
